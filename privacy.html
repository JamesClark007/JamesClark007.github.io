Privacy Policy

Short version: DON'T POP IT has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your device. If you choose to watch an optional ad, our ad partner Google AdMob may collect device and usage information. If you buy "Remove Ads", Apple or Google handles the payment and our purchase provider RevenueCat confirms it.

Who we are

DON'T POP IT ("the app") is published by [Your name or company]. Contact: you@example.com.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, scores, daily-challenge streak and settings (sound, music, vibration, accessibility) are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. They are deleted if you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app may offer an optional rewarded ad: after you run out of moves, you can choose to watch an ad for one extra move. Ads are never shown during gameplay and never without you choosing to watch one.

When an ad is requested, Google AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (where you have allowed it), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type and operating system, and how you interacted with the ad. Google uses this to deliver, measure and, where you have consented, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • On iOS, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking when the app asks.
  • You can limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you buy "Remove Ads", the app does not load the ad service at all.

In-app purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. To confirm and restore your purchase, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives the purchase receipt and a random, anonymous app user ID (not your name or email). See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The app has no user accounts, chat or social features.
  • The app does not include our own analytics. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your permission where required before any data is collected.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law) you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing of your personal information. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, most requests concern our partners above; contact us and we will help you exercise them. You may also contact Google or RevenueCat directly.

Data retention and security

On-device data stays until you reset it or uninstall the app. Our partners keep data according to their own policies linked above. Data sent to partners is transmitted over encrypted connections.
